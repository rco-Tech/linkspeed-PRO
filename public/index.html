How To Test Your USB Cable GUIDE
1
Use a Fast Peripheral

Use a USB 3.2 or USB4 external SSD (e.g., Samsung T7, SanDisk Extreme), an NVMe enclosure, or a 10G USB-C hub as your test device.

2
Connect Through The Cable

Connect the device to your computer using the USB-C cable you want to verify.

3
Read The Negotiated Speed

If it reads 10 Gb/s or 5 Gb/s, your cable has SuperSpeed data lines. If it negotiates at 480 Mb/s, your cable is a charging-only USB 2.0 cable!

About LinkSpeed Pro

Designed for engineers, hardware reviewers, and enthusiasts to inspect physical negotiated bus speeds across USB4, Thunderbolt 3/4, USB 3.2 Gen 2x2, SuperSpeed+ (10G), SuperSpeed (5G), and High-Speed (480M) ports. Instantly flags charge-only cable bottlenecks when 10G/20G peripherals are choked to USB 2.0.
